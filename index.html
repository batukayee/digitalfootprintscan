TL;DR — What is a digital footprint scan?

A digital footprint scan is a process of finding information about you that may be visible online. Start by checking your name, email addresses, phone numbers, usernames, social profiles, people-search listings and data-broker records, then decide what information you want to remove or limit.

How a digital footprint check works

A useful privacy check starts by understanding what information exists and where it may be visible.

1

Identify your information

Start with identifiers such as your name, email addresses, phone numbers and usernames.

2

Find where it appears

Search engines, social platforms, directories, people-search sites and data brokers can reveal different parts of your footprint.

3

Decide what to reduce

Once you understand your exposure, you can decide which information should be removed, limited or monitored.
