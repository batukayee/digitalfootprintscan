Process

How a digital footprint check works

A useful privacy check starts by understanding what information exists and where it may be visible.

Step 01

Find your identifiers

Start with your name, email addresses, phone numbers and usernames.

Step 02

Search where they appear

Check search engines, social platforms, directories, people-search sites and data brokers.

Step 03

Decide what matters

Separate harmless information from details you would rather keep private.

Step 04

Take action

Remove, limit or monitor information where appropriate.
