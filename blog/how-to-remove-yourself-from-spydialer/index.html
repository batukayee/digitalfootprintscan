How to Remove Yourself From SpyDialer (2026 Opt-Out Guide)

SpyDialer is a phone-number lookup site. Here is where its removal process starts and what to know before you begin.

By Digital Footprint ScanUpdated October 12, 2026
TL;DR

Open SpyDialer's opt-out page, click START and follow its removal wizard. The service says it works with U.S. phones only, and businesses use a separate Business Listing Removal page.

SpyDialer is a phone-number lookup service. It states that its results come from public data sources which may be inaccurate, out-of-date or otherwise wrong. Removal is handled by a guided wizard rather than a single form.

How to remove yourself from SpyDialer

1Open the opt-out page

Go to the SpyDialer opt-out page. It says consumers in all states can opt out.

2Click START

The START button opens a guided wizard. The steps inside the wizard load in the tool itself, so we do not describe them here rather than guess.

3Complete the wizard

Follow each prompt and keep a note of the phone number you submit. SpyDialer says its service works with U.S.-based phones only.

4Businesses use a different page

SpyDialer says businesses should use a separate Business Listing Removal page rather than the consumer wizard.

5Recheck later

Look your number up on SpyDialer again after some days and repeat the wizard if it still returns your details.

What we could not confirm: the static opt-out page does not state the wizard's individual steps, any verification method or a processing time. Follow the live prompts and do not rely on third-party instructions that describe steps the tool does not show.

Why phone numbers are a special case

A phone number is often tied to accounts and to scam targeting, so a lookup site makes it easier for strangers to connect a number to a name. Removing the SpyDialer result helps with that site only. For the wider picture, see what an exposed phone number means and how scammers get your phone number and email.

Checking the result

Search SpyDialer for your number and for your name. If the result remains after a reasonable wait, run the wizard again and keep a screenshot. A removed result can also linger in search engines. See why removed information can still appear in search results and how to verify a removal.

Keep going

SpyDialer is one of many sites that republish public data. Continue with How to Remove Yourself From People-Search Sites, and see what to do when removed information keeps reappearing if a number returns.

Frequently asked questions

Can anyone in the U.S. use the SpyDialer opt-out?

SpyDialer says consumers in all states can opt out, and that the service works with U.S.-based phones only.

What if the number belongs to a business?

SpyDialer says businesses should use its separate Business Listing Removal page.

How long does the removal take?

The opt-out page does not state a processing time. Recheck after some days.

Does SpyDialer say its data is accurate?

It says results come from public data sources which may be inaccurate, out-of-date or otherwise wrong.
