Phishing and Smishing Statistics (2026)

How many phishing sites are launched, what phishing and business email compromise cost, and how often organizations and consumers are targeted, from industry trackers and government agencies.

By Digital Footprint ScanUpdated October 12, 202643 statistics · 12 sourcesCite this page
TL;DR

The Anti-Phishing Working Group counted 1,069,681 phishing sites in the second quarter of 2026, up 10.1% on the first quarter. In the FBI’s 2025 data, phishing and spoofing losses were $215.8 million, about three times the 2024 figure, while business email compromise cost $3.05 billion. In the UK, 38% of businesses experienced phishing.

Key takeaways

  • Phishing volume keeps rising. APWG recorded 971,181 phishing sites in Q1 2026 and 1,069,681 in Q2, and June’s 425,808 was the highest month since April 2023 (APWG).
  • FBI-reported losses from phishing and spoofing rose from $70.0 million in 2024 to $215.8 million in 2025, about 3.1 times (our calculation), even though complaints were flat at about 191,000 (IC3).
  • Business email compromise is the costlier cousin: $3.05 billion across 24,768 FBI complaints in 2025.
  • Text and voice channels are growing. APWG saw smishing up 40% and vishing up 20% quarter on quarter in Q2 2026.
  • 38% of UK businesses experienced phishing, and for 69% of organizations that had a breach, phishing was the most disruptive type (UK government survey).

Methodology and limitations

Every figure on this page comes from the organization that published it, such as a regulator, a government agency or the original report. We read the source page or document directly, and we record the period each figure covers. When a number could not be confirmed against a primary source, we left it out. Rows marked “Our calculation” are simple arithmetic on published figures, and the inputs are named in the same row.

  • APWG’s count reflects sites detected and reported, so a rise can reflect better detection as well as more attacks.
  • The IC3 counts complaints that victims chose to file. It does not count all phishing, and the category “phishing and spoofing” is separate from BEC.
  • The UK survey covers organizations that responded, in fieldwork from August to December 2025.

Counts of reports, complaints and notifications measure what was reported or notified, not everything that happened. Currency symbols follow the source. Where an agency does not name its currency, we show the symbol the source uses (for example, C$ for Canada and A$ for Australia are our labels). Figures from different countries use different definitions and should not be added together. Spot an error? Tell us and include the source link; we correct verified errors and note the date at the top of the page.

Frequently asked questions

How many phishing attacks happen each year?

There is no single global count. APWG detected 971,181 phishing sites in Q1 2026 and 1,069,681 in Q2 2026. The FBI received 191,561 phishing and spoofing complaints in 2025 (APWG Phishing Activity Trends, Q1 2026; APWG Phishing Activity Trends, Q2 2026; FBI IC3, 2025 report).

What is smishing?

Smishing is phishing by text message. APWG reported smishing up 40% quarter on quarter in Q2 2026, and vishing (voice phishing) up 20% (APWG Phishing Activity Trends, Q2 2026).

How much money is lost to phishing?

The FBI recorded $215.8 million in reported losses to phishing and spoofing in 2025, up from $70.0 million in 2024, and $3.05 billion to business email compromise (FBI IC3, 2025 report; FBI IC3, 2024 report).

Which industries are targeted most by phishing?

In APWG’s Q2 2026 data, SaaS and webmail brands were the most targeted sector at 29.1% of attacks, followed by payment services at 13.2% and financial institutions at 11.4% (APWG Phishing Activity Trends, Q2 2026).

How can I tell if a message is phishing?

Check the sender and link independently rather than using the message’s own links, and be wary of urgency. Our guide on real breach notices versus phishing has a checklist.
