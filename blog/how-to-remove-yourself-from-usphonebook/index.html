How to Remove Yourself From USPhoneBook (2026 Opt-Out Guide)

USPhoneBook has a dedicated removal workflow that uses email verification and address matching.

By Digital Footprint Scan · Updated September 14, 2026

TL;DR

Use the official removal form, enter your name and email, complete the CAPTCHA, verify through email, match your address details, and submit.

👁 Run a free digital footprint scan to see what else is exposed →

USPhoneBook listings can expose names, phone numbers, addresses and related public-record information. The online removal process is free and can be completed without buying a service.

How to remove yourself from USPhoneBook

1Open the removal page

Go to USPhoneBook’s official removal form.

2Enter your details

Enter your first name, last name and an email address you control. Review and accept the authorization statement.

3Complete the security check

Complete the CAPTCHA/security check and start the removal process.

4Verify by email

Open the privacy-request email and use the button inside it to continue.

5Match your listing

Provide the address details requested by the form so USPhoneBook can identify the correct record.

6Submit and keep the confirmation

Submit the request. USPhoneBook sends progress and completion emails. Keep them so you can confirm when processing is finished.

Important: Removing a USPhoneBook listing does not remove the same information from other people-search sites or public records.

How to verify removal

Search USPhoneBook again after processing. Check the same name, address and phone combinations you used originally, and keep the completion email for your records.

Why information can return

Data sources change and databases refresh. A later source update can create a new listing even after an earlier request succeeded.

Frequently asked questions

Is USPhoneBook removal free?

Yes, the self-service removal process is free.

Do I need an account?

The removal workflow is a privacy request and does not require buying a subscription.

What if I have more than one listing?

Review your search results carefully and follow the current process for each matching listing.
