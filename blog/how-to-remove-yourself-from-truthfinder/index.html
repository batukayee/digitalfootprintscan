How to Remove Yourself From TruthFinder (2026 Opt-Out Guide)

TruthFinder is part of the PeopleConnect network. Here is how to submit a free suppression request and what to expect afterward.

By Digital Footprint ScanUpdated September 14, 2026
TL;DR

Find your matching listing, use the PeopleConnect suppression route, complete email and identity verification, select suppression, and recheck the result after processing.

👁 Run a free digital footprint scan to see what else is exposed →

TruthFinder can expose names, ages, current and past addresses, phone numbers, relatives, associates, and other public-record details. Its suppression system is shared with other PeopleConnect brands.

The important distinction is between suppressing a listing on one service and removing the underlying information from the wider internet. An opt-out affects the service you submit it to; it does not automatically remove the same data from other people-search sites, search engines, public records or data brokers.

How to remove yourself from TruthFinder

1Open the PeopleConnect suppression page

Use TruthFinder’s official opt-out link or go to the PeopleConnect Suppression Center. The request is handled through the shared PeopleConnect system.

2Enter an email address you control

Provide an email address for verification and continue. You do not need to buy a subscription to submit a suppression request.

3Verify your email

Open the verification message from PeopleConnect and follow the link to continue the suppression process.

4Identify your record

Follow the prompts to provide the requested identity details, then locate the matching TruthFinder record.

5Choose suppression

Select the option to suppress the matching record and save the request.

6Wait and verify

PeopleConnect commonly processes suppression within about 48 hours. Search TruthFinder again afterward and repeat the check later if the listing returns.

Important: Keep the confirmation email or request details. If your information returns later, that record can make a repeat request easier.

How to verify removal

After the service’s stated processing period, search your name again using the same location details you used originally. Also check the exact profile URL if you saved it. Search engines can continue displaying an old result for a while even after the source site has suppressed the listing.

Why your information can come back

People-search databases are continually refreshed from public records and other commercial sources. A successful opt-out therefore does not guarantee that a future record will never appear.

Frequently asked questions

Is the TruthFinder opt-out free?

The standard self-service suppression route is intended to be free. You should not need to buy a subscription simply to submit a privacy or opt-out request.

Will removal delete the underlying data everywhere?

No. Suppression affects the service you submit it to. Other sites maintain separate databases and require their own requests.

Can the listing return?

Yes. Source data changes and refreshed public records can create a new or renewed listing.

What if I have multiple listings?

Search for name variations and older addresses. If separate listings appear, follow the current process for each one.

How should I verify removal?

Search the site again after processing and check the old profile URL if you saved it.
