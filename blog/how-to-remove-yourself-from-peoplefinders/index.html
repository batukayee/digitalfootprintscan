How to Remove Yourself From PeopleFinders (2026 Opt-Out Guide)

PeopleFinders provides a free self-service opt-out. The request uses email verification and asks you to identify the exact record you want suppressed.

By Digital Footprint Scan · Updated September 14, 2026

TL;DR

Open the PeopleFinders opt-out page, submit your name and email, complete verification, select your listing, submit the removal, and recheck after processing.

👁 Run a free digital footprint scan to see what else is exposed →

PeopleFinders listings can include names, addresses, phone numbers, relatives and other public-record information. Multiple listings may need separate requests.

How to remove yourself from PeopleFinders

1Open the opt-out page

Go to PeopleFinders’ official opt-out route.

2Enter your name and email

Provide your full name and a working email address. Complete the CAPTCHA/security check if shown.

3Verify the request

Open the email PeopleFinders sends and use its link to continue to the removal form.

4Find the matching record

Search by name and location, then select the listing that belongs to you.

5Submit the removal

Complete the form so the information matches the record and submit the request.

6Wait and verify

PeopleFinders can take several days to process a request. Search again afterward and repeat the process for additional listings.

Important: Suppression affects the PeopleFinders listing. It does not delete the same information from the underlying public records or other brokers.

How to verify removal

Search the site again after processing using the same name and location. Check old profile URLs if you saved them and keep your confirmation email.

Why information can return

People-search databases are refreshed from changing public records and other sources. A new source record can create another listing.

Frequently asked questions

Is PeopleFinders removal free?

The self-service opt-out is free.

How long can removal take?

Processing can take several days, so recheck after the service has had time to process the request.

What if I have multiple records?

Submit separate requests for each matching listing that remains visible.
