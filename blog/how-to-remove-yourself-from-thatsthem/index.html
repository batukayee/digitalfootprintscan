How to Remove Yourself From ThatsThem (2026 Opt-Out Guide)

ThatsThem removes records through a form. Here is what it asks for, what to expect and how to handle search results.

By Digital Footprint ScanUpdated October 12, 2026
TL;DR

Submit the ThatsThem opt-out form with your name, email, address and phone number so it can find your record, and send one request for each record. Expect any search engine copies to take longer to disappear.

Run a free digital footprint scan to see what else is exposed.

ThatsThem is a people-search site. Its opt-out removes your records from its public search. The site is clear that your information may still appear on similar sites, so treat this as one step in a longer cleanup.

How to remove yourself from ThatsThem

1Find your records first

Search ThatsThem for your name and note each record that belongs to you. The site expects a separate request for each record, so a list saves time.

2Open the opt-out form

Use the ThatsThem opt-out page and fill in the form.

3Enter the details it asks for

The form asks for your full name, email address, street address, state, city, ZIP code and phone number. ThatsThem says these fields let it find the correct record or records. Use details that match the record you want removed.

4Submit one request per record

If you find several records with different information for the same person, ThatsThem says you need a request for each one.

5Check the result

ThatsThem says the opt-out removes your records from its public search. Search the site again afterwards to confirm each record is gone.

A privacy trade-off: the form itself asks for contact details. Use an email address you control and only the details that match the record you are removing.

Search engine results

ThatsThem notes that its result pages may have been indexed by search engines. It says it works to have opt-out requests de-indexed, but the process can take more than 5 to 7 days and is outside its control. If a result still appears after that, you may need to contact the search engine's support directly. See why removed information can still appear in search results for how cached copies behave.

What the opt-out page does not say

The page does not describe an identity check, does not state a processing time for the removal itself and does not say whether a record can return. Keep a record of what you submitted and the date, and recheck after a week.

Keep going

Because your details may still appear on similar sites, continue with How to Remove Yourself From People-Search Sites. If a record comes back, see what to do when removed information keeps reappearing and how to verify a removal.

Frequently asked questions

What details does the ThatsThem form need?

Your full name, email address, street address, state, city, ZIP code and phone number, so the site can find the correct record or records.

Do I need to submit one request or several?

One for each record. ThatsThem says records with different information for the same person each need their own request.

Why does my record still show in Google?

Search engines may have indexed the page. ThatsThem says de-indexing can take more than 5 to 7 days and is outside its control.

Will opting out remove me from other sites?

No. ThatsThem says your information may still appear on similar sites.
