How to Remove Yourself From ThatsThem (2026 Opt-Out Guide)

ThatsThem offers a free online opt-out route for suppressing personal information from its people-search results.

By Digital Footprint Scan · Updated September 14, 2026

TL;DR

Find your listing, use the site's official opt-out route, provide the requested contact details, complete verification, and recheck the listing.

👁 Run a free digital footprint scan to see what else is exposed →

ThatsThem can show names, phone numbers, email addresses, addresses and other information assembled from public and commercial sources.

How to remove yourself from ThatsThem

1Find your listing

Search ThatsThem for your name and use location or other details to identify the correct result.

2Open the opt-out route

Use the site’s current opt-out/privacy-removal option and make sure the request is for the correct record.

3Provide contact details

Enter the information the current form requests so the site can process and verify your request.

4Complete verification

Finish any email or security verification required by the form.

5Submit the request

Confirm the removal request and keep any confirmation message or email.

6Recheck the listing

Search again after processing. If the record remains visible, follow the site's current support or opt-out instructions again.

Important: Removing one listing does not remove the same data from other people-search sites or public records.

How to verify removal

Repeat your original search after processing and compare the information carefully. Keep confirmation details in case you need to submit another request.

Why information can return

Source data changes and people-search databases refresh. A new source record can produce a new listing after an earlier opt-out.

Frequently asked questions

Is the ThatsThem opt-out free?

The published self-service opt-out route is free.

Do I need an account?

The opt-out process is intended as a privacy request rather than a paid subscription.

Can a listing return?

Yes. Refreshed source data can create a new record.
