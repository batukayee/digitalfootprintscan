SearchPeopleFree Opt Out: How to Remove Your Profile

SearchPeopleFree uses an email link and a time-limited form. Having the details ready makes the process go quickly.

By Digital Footprint ScanUpdated October 11, 2026
TL;DR

SearchPeopleFree asks for your first and last name, optionally your middle name, plus an email address and captcha, then sends a link valid for 24 hours. After you validate it, you have 30 minutes to complete a form whose details must match the record. Removal takes about three days. It removes you only from this site, which says its data comes from sources such as voter, phone directory and property records.

SearchPeopleFree is a people-search website. Visitors can enter a name and see a profile that may contain age, location, phone numbers and associated people. If you find yourself in the results, the site has an opt-out you can use for free.

What SearchPeopleFree says about its data

The site says its listings draw on public and commercial sources, including voter records, phone directories and property records. That is typical for people-search sites, and it explains why the same details may appear on several. For background, see how people-search sites get your information.

The site also says it is not a consumer reporting agency, so it should not be used to make decisions about employment, credit or housing.

Removal here is not removal everywhere. The opt-out removes your listing from SearchPeopleFree only. The records it draws on, such as voter and property data, still exist at their sources.

Before you start

Have these ready:

  • Your first and last name, and middle name if you want to use it
  • An email address you can open right away
  • The ability to complete a captcha
  • Thirty uninterrupted minutes once the email link arrives

The opt-out steps

1Open the opt-out page

Enter your first and last name, add a middle name if you want, then your email address and complete the captcha. Click the button labeled "Begin removal process".

2Open the email link

The site sends a link that stays valid for 24 hours. Click it to validate your email address.

3Finish the form within 30 minutes

After you validate, complete the form within 30 minutes or the session expires. The details must match the record.

4Save the confirmation

You will see a confirmation page and receive a confirmation email. Keep both.

5Wait about three days

The site says removal takes around three days.

6Clear your cache and search again

After three days, clear your browser cache and search your name. If the listing is still there, submit the opt-out again.

Matching the record

If the form details do not match, the removal may not apply. Check how your name appears in the listing, including spelling, middle initials and suffixes. If you find several listings, repeat the process for each variation. Our guide on searching for variations of your name helps with that.

State privacy rights

The site describes a way to exercise state privacy rights through its form, by phone at 800-958-6869 or by sending a Global Privacy Control signal. Rights depend on your state, so check which law applies to you before relying on this option.

Why a profile can return

The site notes that records may reappear, and a periodic refresh is a sensible habit. Because the underlying records stay public at their source, new copies can be created later. If that happens, see what to do when a data broker removal request fails and what to do when removed information keeps reappearing.

What to do next

Work through the other sites that list you. Another guide covers SmartBackgroundChecks, and the overview of people-search opt-outs lists the wider process.

Common reasons the form fails

Most failed attempts come down to a handful of causes. The email link may have expired after 24 hours. The 30-minute session may have run out because the form was left open. The details you entered may not match the record, for example a different spelling or a missing middle name. The browser may be showing a cached copy of the listing even though it has been removed. Fix the cause, then try again. Keep your confirmation email, since it records when you first asked.

Frequently asked questions

Is SearchPeopleFree removal free?

Yes. The opt-out does not ask for payment.

What happens if I miss the 30-minute window?

The session expires. Start the process again from the opt-out page.

How long does removal take?

The site says about three days. Clear your cache and recheck after that.

Does this remove me from other sites?

No. It removes you from SearchPeopleFree only.

What if I am still listed after three days?

Clear your cache, search again, and submit the opt-out again if the listing remains.
