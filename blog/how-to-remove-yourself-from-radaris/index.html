How to Remove Yourself From Radaris (2026 Opt-Out Guide)

Radaris provides profile-management and privacy controls for people who want their public listing suppressed.

By Digital Footprint Scan · Updated September 14, 2026

TL;DR

Find your Radaris profile, open its current privacy/profile-control route, complete verification, request suppression, and recheck after processing.

👁 Run a free digital footprint scan to see what else is exposed →

Radaris profiles can contain names, addresses, phone numbers, relatives, associates and other public-record information. The exact removal flow can change as the site updates its profile-management tools.

How to remove yourself from Radaris

1Find your Radaris profile

Search Radaris for your name and location and open the profile that matches you.

2Open the privacy/profile-control route

Use Radaris’s current privacy or profile-management link rather than relying on an old third-party form.

3Complete verification

Provide the identifying or contact information requested by the current form and complete any verification.

4Request suppression

Choose the available option to hide or remove the matching profile and submit it.

5Save confirmation

Keep any confirmation page, email or request identifier provided by Radaris.

6Recheck your profile

Search again after processing. If information has returned, use the current profile-control process again.

Important: Radaris suppression affects the Radaris profile. It does not erase the underlying public records or other broker databases.

How to verify removal

Repeat the same name and location search after processing. Check the original profile if you saved its URL and keep your confirmation.

Why information can return

Source data changes and databases refresh. A later public-record update can produce another profile.

Frequently asked questions

Is Radaris removal free?

The self-service privacy/profile-control process is intended for consumers to use without buying a removal subscription.

Does suppression delete public records?

No. It affects the Radaris profile rather than the underlying source records.

Can a Radaris profile return?

Yes. New or refreshed source data can result in another profile.
