How to Remove Yourself From MyLife (2026 Opt-Out Guide)

MyLife provides a privacy-request workflow for removing or suppressing a reputation profile. Here is the current self-service route.

By Digital Footprint Scan · Updated September 14, 2026

TL;DR

Find your MyLife profile, open the privacy request form, provide your identifying details, verify your email, paste the profile link, complete the CAPTCHA, and submit.

👁 Run a free digital footprint scan to see what else is exposed →

MyLife profiles can include names, locations, age information, contact details and other information assembled from public and commercial sources.

How to remove yourself from MyLife

1Find your MyLife profile

Search MyLife for your first and last name and identify the profile that belongs to you.

2Open the privacy request form

Use MyLife’s FAQ/privacy area and select the current option for having a Reputation Profile removed.

3Provide identifying details

The current form may ask for your name, location and birth year so it can match the correct profile.

4Verify your email

Enter an email address you control and complete the email-verification step.

5Provide the profile link

Paste the URL of your MyLife profile into the request form.

6Complete the security check and submit

Finish the CAPTCHA/security step and submit the request. Keep the confirmation and recheck your profile afterward.

Important: A MyLife profile removal affects MyLife. It does not automatically remove the same information from other sites or public records.

How to verify removal

Search MyLife again after processing and check the old profile URL if you saved it. Keep your confirmation email for your records.

Why information can return

People-search databases are refreshed from changing data sources. A later update can create a new or changed profile.

Frequently asked questions

Is MyLife removal free?

The self-service privacy-request route is available without buying a removal subscription.

Do I need my profile URL?

The current request form asks for the link to the profile being removed, so save it before starting.

Can a profile return?

Yes. Refreshed source data can result in a new profile.
