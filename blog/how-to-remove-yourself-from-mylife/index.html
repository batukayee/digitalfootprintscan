MyLife Opt Out: How to Remove Your Public Profile (2026)

MyLife does not offer a one-click opt-out. Its privacy policy describes an email or Help-section request, plus a separate Do Not Sell form.

By Siddharth ShankarUpdated October 26, 2026
TL;DR

MyLife's privacy policy says to request removal of a public profile through its HELP section or by emailing membersupport@mylife.com. It verifies that you own the profile, then removes it from public display, though some data may remain in backups. Separately, you can opt out of the sale of your data at mylife.com/privacyrequest. Privacy-law requests get a response within 45 days.

MyLife is a people-search and profile site that publishes "Public Profiles" built from licensed public-domain information. The operator named in its privacy policy, updated August 20, 2026, is InsightBridge LLC. The policy says profiles can include public records about real estate transactions, criminal history, court cases, traffic infractions and civil lawsuits, as well as age, contact details, professional information and marital status.

Unlike many sites, MyLife does not describe a simple online opt-out form for removing a profile. The routes below are the ones its own privacy policy sets out.

How this guide was checked: we read MyLife's official opt-out page (the MyLife privacy policy) on October 26, 2026 and wrote the steps from it. Sites change their forms, so follow the live prompts if anything looks different.

Route 1: Ask MyLife to remove your public profile

1Find your profile and save its address

Search MyLife or Google for your name and open the MyLife profile that matches you. Copy its web address; it helps MyLife find the right profile.

2Send the removal request

MyLife's privacy policy says to visit its HELP section or email membersupport@mylife.com to request removal of a Member Profile or Public Profile. By email, include your full name, the profile address and a clear request to remove the profile from public display.

3Complete MyLife's verification

MyLife says it takes reasonable steps to confirm you are the owner of the profile and may contact you for confirmation. Reply promptly and share only what is needed to prove the profile is yours.

4Check the profile later

MyLife says it will take reasonable steps to remove the profile from public display after verification. Load the saved address again after a few weeks. Keep a copy of your email in case you need to follow up.

Route 2: Opt out of the sale of your information

MyLife's policy says it shares or sells personal information with customers, marketers, retailers, online platforms and data brokers. Removing your public profile does not necessarily stop that. California residents can opt out of sale through the Do Not Sell My Personal Information page at mylife.com/privacyrequest or by email. The policy also lists residents of Colorado, Connecticut, Delaware, Indiana, Iowa, Kentucky, Maryland, Minnesota, Montana, Nebraska, New Hampshire, New Jersey, Oregon, Rhode Island, Tennessee, Texas, Utah and Virginia as having state privacy rights, including deletion and opting out of sale, exercised by emailing membersupport@mylife.com.

How long MyLife has to respond

For privacy-law requests, MyLife says it will respond within 45 days, extendable once by another 45 days if it tells you why. If it declines, the policy says you may be able to appeal by email, and it will reply within 60 days of an appeal.

What MyLife says it keeps

  • Backups. After removing a profile from public display, MyLife says some information may remain in its backup systems.
  • Suppression data. For California opt-outs, MyLife explains that "do not sell" requests let it keep your information for suppression purposes, so it is not sold again.
  • Data not collected from you. MyLife notes that deletion rights apply to information collected from you, which it says does not cover much of its database.

Claiming a profile instead

MyLife also lets people claim their public profile after verifying their identity, then edit or update what is shown. That can fix wrong information, but it keeps a profile online under your name. If your goal is to disappear from people-search results, ask for removal rather than claiming.

Next steps

MyLife says its data comes from public databases, aggregators, social platforms and other third parties, so the same details are likely on other sites. Continue with our opt-out routine, and if you live in a state with a privacy law, see how to send a deletion request.

Frequently asked questions

Can I remove my MyLife profile online?

MyLife's privacy policy directs removal requests to its HELP section or to membersupport@mylife.com, followed by ownership verification.

Is MyLife removal free?

The privacy policy does not describe a fee for removing a public profile. For some state privacy requests it says a reasonable fee may be charged where the law allows.

Does removing my MyLife profile delete all my data?

Not necessarily. MyLife says some information may remain in backups, and opting out of sale is a separate request.

How long does MyLife take to respond?

For privacy-law requests, MyLife says within 45 days, with one possible 45-day extension.

Who runs MyLife?

Its privacy policy names InsightBridge LLC, based in Los Angeles.
