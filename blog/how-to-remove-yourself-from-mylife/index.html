How to Remove Yourself From MyLife (2026 Opt-Out Guide)

MyLife markets itself as a reputation platform. Here is how to find your profile and submit a removal request.

By Digital Footprint ScanUpdated October 12, 2026
TL;DR

Find your MyLife profile and copy its URL, open the Do Not Sell My Personal Information form, paste the URL with your name, state and email, complete the CAPTCHA and click Opt Out. Guides disagree on timing, so recheck after a few weeks.

Run a free digital footprint scan to see what else is exposed.

MyLife describes itself as a reputation platform, and one guide says profiles can carry a public Reputation Score built from background details, social media and reviews. Both guides we checked list work and education history, court records, photos and contact details among the information shown.

How this guide was checked: we could not open this site's own opt-out page from our systems. The steps below come from third-party guides (DeleteMe and Optery) updated between July and September 2026, and we kept only the steps that more than one guide describes. Those publishers sell removal services, so treat them as a cross-check, not an official source. Where the guides differ, we say so. Follow the live prompts if the site looks different.

How to remove yourself from MyLife

1Find your profile

On the MyLife home page, scroll to the bottom, click the first letter of your last name, then narrow the name list until you find your profile. Both guides describe this alphabetical route.

2Copy the profile URL

Open your profile, close any pop-up that covers it and copy the web address from your browser. The removal form asks for it.

3Open the removal form

Use the Do Not Sell My Personal Information link in the MyLife footer. One guide gives the direct address mylife.com/privacyrequest.

4Fill in the form

Enter your name, state, the profile URL and an email address, then complete the CAPTCHA. The California box is only for California residents. One guide suggests a disposable email address.

5Click Opt Out

Submit the form. The guides then differ. One says you must verify by clicking a link in an email and that the listing should go within 15 business days. The other describes only an on-screen confirmation and gives no timeframe.

Where the guides differ: because they disagree on email verification and timing, watch your inbox for a verification message and keep it. Recheck your profile address after three weeks.

Checking the result

After the waiting period, load the profile address you saved and search for your name again. If it is still there, submit the form again and keep a screenshot. See how to verify a removal and why removed information can still appear in search results.

Why a profile can come back

Neither guide states how often MyLife relists profiles, but both say people-search data changes constantly. Check again every few months, and see what to do when removed information keeps reappearing.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need the profile URL?

Both guides say to copy it from your profile and paste it into the form, which is how the form identifies your record.

How long does MyLife removal take?

One guide says within 15 business days after you verify by email. The other gives no timeframe.

Will MyLife email me?

One guide says you must click a verification link in an email. Check your inbox and spam folder.

Does removal fix my other profiles?

No. It affects MyLife only. Other people-search sites need their own opt-outs.
