How to Remove Yourself From Nuwber (2026 Opt-Out Guide)

Nuwber removes a profile once you submit its address and confirm by email. Here are the steps both guides agree on.

By Digital Footprint ScanUpdated October 12, 2026
TL;DR

Search Nuwber, open your record with View Details, copy its URL, click Remove My Info at the bottom of the page, submit it and confirm by email. One guide says removal takes about 24 hours.

Run a free digital footprint scan to see what else is exposed.

Nuwber is a people-search site. Both guides list names, aliases, age or birth date, current and past addresses, phone numbers, email addresses and relatives among its free results, and one adds neighbors, social profiles and property details.

How this guide was checked: we could not open this site's own opt-out page from our systems. The steps below come from third-party guides (DeleteMe and Optery) updated between July and September 2026, and we kept only the steps that more than one guide describes. Those publishers sell removal services, so treat them as a cross-check, not an official source. Where the guides differ, we say so. Follow the live prompts if the site looks different.

How to remove yourself from Nuwber

1Search for yourself

Go to nuwber.com and search by name and city or state. You can also search by phone number or address.

2Open your record

Click the View Details button on your record. One guide warns against the green Get Full Report button on other records, since it leads to a different site.

3Copy the profile URL

Copy the address from your browser. The form can pre-fill it, but confirm it matches the record you want removed.

4Use Remove My Info

Scroll to the bottom of the profile page and click Remove My Info in the footer, then click Opt Out on the removal page.

5Enter an email address and confirm

Enter an email address and click Remove. Both guides suggest a temporary address. Open the email from Nuwber and click Confirm Request. Check spam if it does not arrive.

How long it takes

DeleteMe reports that the information should be removed in about 24 hours. Optery gives no timeframe. Recheck after a day or two, and again after a week.

Checking the result

Search Nuwber again for your name and any variations, and open the profile address you saved. Old copies can stay in search engines for a while. See why removed information can still appear in search results and how to verify a removal.

If your record comes back

Nuwber gathers data from public sources, so it can list you again. DeleteMe advises rechecking regularly and repeating the opt-out. See what to do when removed information keeps reappearing.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to confirm by email?

Yes. Both guides describe clicking Confirm Request in an email from Nuwber.

How long does Nuwber take?

DeleteMe says about 24 hours. Optery gives no timeframe.

What is the green Get Full Report button?

One guide warns it leads to a different site, so use View Details instead.

Can I be listed again?

Yes. Nuwber collects public data over time, so repeat the opt-out if you reappear.
