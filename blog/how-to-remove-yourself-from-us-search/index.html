How to Remove Yourself From US Search (2026 Opt-Out Guide)

US Search is part of the PeopleConnect network, so its suppression request is handled through a shared privacy system.

By Digital Footprint Scan · Updated September 14, 2026

TL;DR

Open the official US Search opt-out route, verify your email, identify your record, select suppression, and recheck after processing.

US Search can surface names, current and historical addresses, phone numbers, relatives, associates and other public-record information. It is part of PeopleConnect, which operates several related people-search brands.

How to remove yourself from US Search

1Open the official suppression page

Start from US Search’s current opt-out link or the PeopleConnect Suppression Center.

2Submit your email

Enter an email address you control and continue.

3Verify your email

Use the verification message sent by PeopleConnect to enter the suppression workflow.

4Locate your record

Provide the identity details requested by the current form and find the record that matches you.

5Select suppression

Choose the suppression option for the matching record and save the request.

6Check again

Allow roughly 48 hours for processing, then search US Search again. Check other PeopleConnect brands too because the same data network can feed multiple sites.

Important: An opt-out suppresses the service listing; it does not erase public records or automatically remove the same information elsewhere.

How to verify removal

Search your name again after the processing period and compare the result with the original record. Keep the confirmation email in case you need to repeat the request.

Why your information can return

People-search databases refresh from public records and commercial sources. A later refresh can produce a new or changed listing.

Frequently asked questions

Is US Search removal free?

The self-service privacy/suppression route is intended to be free.

Does it remove me from every related site?

PeopleConnect shares a suppression system, but recheck each relevant service after processing.

Can a listing return?

Yes. Refreshed source data can create a new listing.
