How to Remove Yourself From Instant Checkmate (2026 Opt-Out Guide)

Instant Checkmate uses the PeopleConnect suppression system. One request can cover several PeopleConnect brands.

By Digital Footprint Scan · Updated September 14, 2026

TL;DR

Use Instant Checkmate’s official opt-out route, verify your email, identify the matching record, choose suppression, and recheck after processing.

👁 Run a free digital footprint scan to see what else is exposed →

Instant Checkmate reports can expose names, addresses, phone numbers, relatives, associates and public-record information. The service is part of PeopleConnect, which operates several related people-search brands.

How to remove yourself from Instant Checkmate

1Open the official opt-out page

Start at Instant Checkmate’s opt-out page. Its privacy-removal flow leads into the PeopleConnect suppression system.

2Enter your email

Submit an email address you control and continue through the verification flow.

3Verify the email

Open the verification email from PeopleConnect and follow its link to continue.

4Provide identity details

The current flow may ask for your date of birth and legal name so PeopleConnect can locate your record.

5Select suppression

Find the matching profile, choose the suppression behavior, and save the request.

6Recheck the network

Allow about 48 hours, then search Instant Checkmate again. Because PeopleConnect operates several brands, check related services too.

Important: Suppression is not the same as deleting the underlying public-record data. Other sites may still have the same information.

How to verify removal

Search the service again after the processing period and compare the result with your original record. Save confirmation emails so a repeat request is easier if your information returns.

Why your information can come back

People-search databases are refreshed from public records and other sources. A later data refresh can create a new or changed listing even after a successful suppression.

Frequently asked questions

Is the opt-out free?

The self-service privacy/suppression route is intended to be free.

Does it remove me from every PeopleConnect site?

PeopleConnect uses a shared suppression system, but you should still recheck each relevant brand and follow its current instructions.

Can the listing return?

Yes. Refreshed source data can result in a new listing.

What if I have multiple records?

Review all matching records and follow the current process for each one that belongs to you.
