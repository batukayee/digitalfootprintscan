How to Remove Yourself From Instant Checkmate (2026 Guide)

Instant Checkmate removals run through PeopleConnect's Suppression Center. Here are the steps both guides agree on.

By Digital Footprint ScanUpdated October 12, 2026
TL;DR

Open the Suppression Center from Instant Checkmate's footer, verify your email, enter your date of birth and name, choose your record, verify with a code and set it to Suppressed. Neither guide states a processing time.

Instant Checkmate is a background-report service. Guides say its reports can show names, aliases, age, phone numbers, email addresses, location history, relatives and associates, and criminal and traffic records. PeopleConnect manages its opt-out, using the same system as Intelius and TruthFinder.

How this guide was checked: we could not open this site's own opt-out page from our systems. The steps below come from third-party guides (DeleteMe and Optery) updated between July and September 2026, and we kept only the steps that more than one guide describes. Those publishers sell removal services, so treat them as a cross-check, not an official source. Where the guides differ, we say so. Follow the live prompts if the site looks different.

How to remove yourself from Instant Checkmate

1Open the Suppression Center

Scroll to the footer of the site and click Do Not Sell Or Share My Personal Information. Expand View Public Data Tools and click Manage My Suppression Rules.

2Enter your email address

Enter an email address, tick the box agreeing to the Terms of Use and Privacy Policy and click Continue. One guide suggests a disposable address.

3Verify your email

Open the email from PeopleConnect and click Verify Email. One guide says the link expires after 15 minutes, so do this straight away.

4Enter your date of birth and name

Select your date of birth, which one guide warns you cannot change afterwards, then enter your first, middle (optional) and last legal name. Use add another name if you have used others.

5Choose your record

Select the record that matches you from the list and continue.

6Verify with a code

Choose email or phone, enter the email address or phone number shown under Verification and click Request Verification Code. Enter the code you receive.

7Set the record to Suppressed

Under Desired Behavior, change the setting from Displayed to Suppressed and click Save.

No further confirmation. DeleteMe says the only confirmation is the word Suppressed in the Desired Behavior field. Take a screenshot before you leave the page.

How long it takes

Neither guide states a processing time. Recheck Instant Checkmate after a few days by searching your name and location, and compare the result with your screenshot.

What suppression does and does not do

Suppression hides your record from Instant Checkmate's displayed results. It does not remove the public records the report is built from, and it does not cover other companies' sites. See why removed information can still appear in search results and how to verify a removal.

If your record returns

DeleteMe says suppression may not be permanent, because a site can list you again when it finds more information. Recheck regularly and repeat the process. See what to do when removed information keeps reappearing.

Frequently asked questions

Who runs the Instant Checkmate opt-out?

Both guides say PeopleConnect manages it through its Suppression Center.

Do I need to give my date of birth?

Yes. Both guides describe entering your date of birth to find your record. DeleteMe warns you cannot change it afterwards.

How do I know it worked?

DeleteMe says the only confirmation is Suppressed showing in the Desired Behavior field.

Does this cover other PeopleConnect sites?

Neither guide says it does. Check each site separately.
