How to Remove Yourself From FastPeopleSearch (2026 Opt-Out Guide)

FastPeopleSearch exposes searchable contact and people records. Here is the current free removal process, including the listing search and deletion confirmation flow.

By Digital Footprint ScanUpdated September 14, 2026
TL;DR

Open fastpeoplesearch.com/removal, find your record, open the matching listing, and confirm the removal request. The process is free and usually takes only a few minutes, but a new profile can appear after future data refreshes.

FastPeopleSearch is a people-search site that makes names, addresses, phone numbers and related information searchable. It can create a listing even when you have never registered with the service.

The important part of the opt-out is selecting the exact record that belongs to you. If the site has several records connected to different locations or identifiers, those may need separate attention.

What a FastPeopleSearch profile can show

A FastPeopleSearch result may contain:

  • Full name and name variations
  • Current and previous addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Possible relatives and associates
  • Age-related information
  • Other public-record details

Where FastPeopleSearch gets this information

FastPeopleSearch compiles information from public and other commercially available sources. Records can be combined into profiles, which is why outdated contact information and former addresses may remain associated with a person.

How to remove yourself from FastPeopleSearch

1Open the removal tool

Go to fastpeoplesearch.com/removal. This is the site’s dedicated removal page.

2Search for your listing

Use your name and location to find the record that belongs to you. Review the information carefully, especially addresses and phone numbers, because common names can produce multiple results.

3Select the matching record

Open the record that matches your identity. Do not select a similar-looking record belonging to another person.

4Confirm the removal

Use the removal or deletion control associated with your record and follow the prompts to confirm that you want the listing suppressed.

5Complete any verification requested

If the removal flow presents a security check or confirmation prompt, complete it before leaving the page. The exact interface can vary as FastPeopleSearch updates its privacy tools.

6Wait and recheck

Give the site time to process the request, then search again using your name and location. If the same listing remains visible after a reasonable processing period, repeat the removal flow for that specific record.

How to confirm it worked

Search FastPeopleSearch again after the removal request has had time to process. The matching record should no longer be available publicly. If a search engine still shows an old FastPeopleSearch result, check whether the source page itself has already disappeared before treating it as a failed removal.

FastPeopleSearch can relist information. People-search databases refresh their records and can create a new listing from new or updated sources. A successful removal today does not guarantee permanent suppression.

Does removing FastPeopleSearch remove me from other sites too?

No. A FastPeopleSearch removal controls your FastPeopleSearch exposure. It does not automatically remove matching information from other people-search services, data brokers, public records or search engines.

FastPeopleSearch opt-out FAQ

Is FastPeopleSearch removal free?

Yes. The site provides a public removal tool without requiring a paid subscription.

Do I need an account?

No paid membership is required for the standard public removal process.

How long does removal take?

Processing time can vary. Recheck the exact listing after the request has had time to take effect rather than assuming an immediate disappearance.

What if there are several records for me?

Review and remove each matching record that actually belongs to you.

Can FastPeopleSearch list me again?

Yes. New data sources or database refreshes can produce a new or refreshed listing later.

Does removal erase public records?

No. It removes the FastPeopleSearch listing, not the underlying public records.
