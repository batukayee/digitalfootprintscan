How we compared them

We compared each service on the things that decide whether it is worth paying for:

  • Where it works. A service that cannot act for residents of your country is no use to you, however good it is.
  • Coverage. How many data brokers and people-search sites it handles, and whether you can ask for sites outside its list.
  • Repeat removals. Brokers re-add people, so a one-off request is not enough.
  • Price for one person per year, and whether there is a free plan or a refund window.
  • Extras you may or may not need, such as identity insurance or a VPN.

Prices come from each provider's own pricing pages on the date shown at the top, apart from DeleteMe, whose prices we took from its published plan list as reported by independent reviewers. Providers change prices often, so the price at checkout is the one that counts. Rankings are editorial and are not paid for.

Do you need to pay at all?

No. Everything these services do, you can do yourself for free: find your listings, submit each site's opt-out form, and check back every few months. The catch is time. There are hundreds of brokers, and many re-add people after a while. These guides walk you through it.

Questions people ask

Do data removal services actually work?

They work for the sites they cover. They send legally backed removal requests and repeat them when brokers re-add you. They cannot remove information from sites outside their list, from news articles, or from public records at the source, and they cannot delete a breach that has already happened.

Which data removal service works outside the US?

Incogni covers the UK, Canada and the EU as well as the US. DeleteMe also offers service in the UK, Canada, Australia and some European countries. NordVPN's Ultra plan, which includes Incogni, is sold in Canada and selected European countries. Optery, Aura and EasyOptOuts focus on the US.

Is a VPN a data removal service?

No. A VPN hides your connection, not the details brokers already hold about you. The exception in this comparison is NordVPN's Ultra plan, which bundles Incogni data removal with the VPN.

Will my information come back after removal?

Often, yes. Brokers buy fresh data and re-create profiles. That is why ongoing services repeat their requests. If you do it yourself, recheck every few months. See what to do when removed information reappears.
