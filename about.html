About Digital Footprint Scan

Helping people understand their online exposure.

Digital Footprint Scan is an independent privacy information resource focused on helping people understand what personal information may be exposed online and what practical steps they can take to reduce it.

What we cover

Your digital footprint can extend far beyond the social profiles you actively use. Names, email addresses, phone numbers, usernames, professional information, directory listings and other records may appear across different parts of the internet.

We create practical guides covering topics such as digital footprint checks, personal information exposure, email exposure, phone-number exposure, data brokers and people-search websites.

Our approach

Our goal is to make privacy research easier to understand and easier to act on. We focus on clear explanations, practical steps and transparent information rather than unnecessary fear or sensational claims.

When discussing privacy services, we aim to explain what a service does, who it may be suitable for, and any important limitations that readers should consider.

Independent information

Digital Footprint Scan is not a government agency, security company or law-enforcement service. The website provides educational information and research to help visitors make more informed privacy decisions.

Important: Digital Footprint Scan does not currently claim to perform an automated scan of a visitor's personal information. Our current resources explain how people can investigate and reduce their online exposure.

Affiliate transparency

Some pages may contain links to third-party privacy services. If a visitor purchases a service through an affiliate link, Digital Footprint Scan may receive a commission at no additional cost to the visitor.

Affiliate relationships do not change the importance of providing useful information about the services and their limitations.

Privacy matters

Understanding where your information appears online is an important first step toward making better privacy decisions. We aim to help visitors identify potential exposure and understand the options available for reducing it.
