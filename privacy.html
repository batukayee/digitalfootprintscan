Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us.

This Privacy Policy explains how Digital Footprint Scan handles information relating to visitors to this website.

Information we collect

Digital Footprint Scan is currently an informational website. We do not currently require visitors to create accounts or submit personal information in order to read our content.

If you contact us, information you voluntarily provide may be used solely to respond to your message and handle the communication.

Website usage and analytics

We may use website analytics or similar technologies to understand general website usage, such as which pages receive visits and how visitors interact with the site.

Analytics services may collect technical information such as browser type, device information, approximate location and pages visited. The information collected depends on the services actually enabled on the website.

Cookies

This website may use cookies or similar technologies provided by analytics, advertising or affiliate services.

Cookies may be used to remember preferences, measure website activity or attribute referrals to an affiliate partner.

Affiliate links

Some pages on Digital Footprint Scan may contain affiliate links to third-party products or services.

When a visitor follows an affiliate link and makes a qualifying purchase or completes another qualifying action, we may receive a commission at no additional cost to the visitor.

Affiliate tracking may involve cookies or other referral technologies operated by the relevant third-party provider or affiliate network.

Third-party websites

Our website may link to third-party websites, including privacy services, data-removal providers and other resources.

Once you leave Digital Footprint Scan, the privacy practices and policies of the third-party website apply. We recommend reviewing those policies before providing personal information or purchasing a service.

Data security

We take reasonable steps to maintain the security of information handled through this website. However, no internet transmission or online service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Children's privacy

This website is intended for a general audience and is not directed specifically toward children. We do not knowingly request personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the website, technology or services we use. The updated version will be published on this page.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how this website handles information, please use our contact page.
